# Week 3 — Project 3 Completion: Customer Segmentation Analysis

## Deliverables
- `Project3_Customer_Segmentation.ipynb` — full, executed, reproducible notebook: EDA, RFM
  engineering, Elbow/Silhouette analysis, K-Means clustering (K=5), cluster visualizations,
  and business recommendations per segment.
- `customer_segments.csv` — final output: every customer with their RFM values, assigned
  cluster, and segment label (Champions, Loyal Customers, Potential Loyalists, At Risk,
  Lost/Hibernating).

## Summary of Findings
Five behaviorally distinct customer segments were identified with a Silhouette Score
validating good cluster separation. Each segment has a tailored marketing recommendation
(see the notebook's final sections) — e.g. loyalty rewards for Champions vs. low-cost
reactivation campaigns for the Lost/Hibernating segment.
